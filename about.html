Science Meets Natural Beauty

Founded in 2020 by Soni V., Soni Cosmetics was born out of a passion for clean, high-performance skincare and makeup. We believe that beauty shouldn't be a compromise between health and results.

Our lab in Mumbai focuses on marrying traditional botanical wisdom with modern dermatological science to create formulations that truly transform your skin from within.

Our Mission

To empower every individual with confidence through high-quality, cruelty-free, and dermatologically tested cosmetic products that celebrate diverse beauty.

Our Vision

To be the global leader in conscious beauty, setting the standard for ingredient transparency and sustainable luxury in the cosmetic industry.

The Soni Commitment

Clean Ingredients

Formulated without parabens, sulfates, or harmful synthetics. Just pure skin goodness.

Cruelty Free

We never test on animals. Every product is ethically manufactured and 100% cruelty-free.

Science Backed

Every drop is rigorously tested for stability and efficacy by our team of experts.

Eco Packaging

We use recyclable glass and responsibly sourced materials to minimize our footprint.
